Weather glossary explained.

What does dew point, jet stream or wind chill actually mean? Every weather term used in a forecast, explained clearly and linked to related terms and full articles.

Glossary

Air Quality Index (AQI)

A standardised scale, usually 0 to 500, that reports how polluted the air is and what health precautions, if any, are advisable.

Glossary

Barometric Pressure

The weight of the atmosphere pressing down at a given location, measured in units such as hectopascals or inches of mercury.

Read more →
Glossary

Cold Front

The leading edge of an advancing mass of cooler air, which typically brings a sharp, short burst of rain or storms as it passes.

Read more →
Glossary

Dew Point

The temperature at which air becomes saturated with moisture and water vapour starts to condense into dew, fog or clouds.

Read more →
Glossary

El Niño and La Niña

Opposite phases of a recurring Pacific Ocean pattern that shifts global weather by changing sea-surface temperatures near the equator.

Read more →
Glossary

Fog

A cloud that forms at ground level, reducing visibility to less than roughly one kilometre.

Read more →
Glossary

Frost

A thin layer of ice crystals that forms on surfaces when the air temperature at ground level drops to 0°C or below.

Read more →
Glossary

Heat Index

A single number that combines air temperature and humidity to estimate how hot it actually feels to the human body.

Read more →
Glossary

High-Pressure System

An area where atmospheric pressure is higher than its surroundings, usually bringing settled, drier weather.

Read more →
Glossary

Isobar

A line on a weather map connecting points of equal atmospheric pressure, used to visualise high- and low-pressure systems.

Read more →
Glossary

Jet Stream

A narrow band of fast-moving air high in the atmosphere that steers weather systems across the mid-latitudes.

Read more →
Glossary

Low-Pressure System

An area where atmospheric pressure is lower than its surroundings, typically associated with clouds, rain and unsettled weather.

Read more →
Glossary

Monsoon

A seasonal reversal of wind direction that brings a distinct wet season to large parts of Asia, West Africa and northern Australia.

Read more →
Glossary

Occluded Front

A weather front formed when a faster-moving cold front catches up with a slower warm front, lifting the warm air between them off the ground.

Read more →
Glossary

Polar Vortex

A large, persistent area of low pressure and cold air that circles each of Earth's poles, strongest in winter.

Read more →
Glossary

Precipitation

Any form of water, liquid or frozen, that falls from clouds to the ground, including rain, snow, sleet and hail.

Read more →
Glossary

Relative Humidity

The amount of moisture in the air expressed as a percentage of the maximum the air could hold at that temperature.

Read more →
Glossary

Thunderstorm

A storm producing lightning and thunder, usually formed by rapidly rising warm, moist air building into a towering cumulonimbus cloud.

Read more →
Glossary

UV Index

A scale, typically 0 to 11+, describing the strength of ultraviolet radiation from the sun and the sunburn risk it poses.

Read more →
Glossary

Visibility

The greatest distance at which an object can still be clearly seen against the sky, used to describe how fog, rain, snow or haze affect sight.

Read more →
Glossary

Warm Front

The leading edge of an advancing mass of warmer air, which typically brings a longer period of lighter, steadier rain than a cold front.

Read more →
Glossary

Wind Chill

A measure of how much colder moving air makes a given temperature feel, by speeding up the rate the body loses heat.
