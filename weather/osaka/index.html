Practical weather notes for Osaka

Osaka has clearly defined seasons, with hot, humid summers affected by the same East Asian rainy season, tsuyu, that moves across Japan in June and early July, followed by a second wet spell if a typhoon tracks close to the Kansai region between August and October.

Seasons in Osaka

Winters are cool rather than cold, with occasional light snow but nothing like the heavy snowfall seen on Japan's northwestern coast, since Osaka sits on the more sheltered Pacific side of the country. Spring and autumn are generally mild and drier, and are the seasons most associated with cherry blossoms and autumn foliage respectively.

Best time to visit Osaka

The warmest month in Osaka is August, with an average high of 32.4°C (90°F). The coolest is January, at 9.0°C (48°F). The wettest month is July, with about 252 mm (9.9 in) of rain. Highs of 25°C (77°F) or more are typical from June to September. For sightseeing and walking, with highs between 18 and 27°C (64 to 81°F), April to June and October are the most comfortable. The sea is warmest in August, at 30.1°C (86°F), and coolest in February, at 8.3°C (47°F). It is above 21°C (70°F), comfortable for swimming, from June to October.

Climate FAQ

What is the warmest month in Osaka?
August, with an average high of 32.4°C (90°F) and an average low of 25.2°C (77°F) (averages for 2015-2024).

What is the wettest month in Osaka?
July, with about 252 mm (9.9 in) of rain. The driest month is December.

When can you swim in the sea in Osaka?
The sea is above 21°C (70°F) from June to October, and warmest in August at 30.1°C (86°F).
