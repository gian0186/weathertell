Practical weather notes for Amsterdam

Amsterdam's flat, open position close to the North Sea means wind is almost always part of the picture, and it can make a mild-looking temperature feel noticeably colder. Rain tends to arrive in short, fast-moving showers rather than settling in for the whole day.

Seasons in Amsterdam

Winters are mild for the latitude but often grey and damp, while summers are rarely very hot. Whatever the season, a layer that blocks wind is usually more useful here than one that blocks cold.

Frequently asked questions

Is the 14-day forecast reliable?
The first few days are usually the most accurate. Further ahead, the forecast mainly shows a trend rather than the exact weather at a specific hour.

What should I check before heading outside?
Look at the combination of rain, wind and feels-like temperature. A short trip can handle a passing shower, but a longer walk or bike ride benefits from a more stable stretch of weather.

Best time to visit Amsterdam

The warmest month in Amsterdam is August, with an average high of 22.0°C (72°F). The coolest is January, at 6.7°C (44°F). The wettest month is October, with about 92 mm (3.6 in) of rain. Average highs stay below 25°C (77°F) all year. For sightseeing and walking, with highs between 18 and 27°C (64 to 81°F), June to September are the most comfortable.

Climate FAQ

What is the warmest month in Amsterdam?
August, with an average high of 22.0°C (72°F) and an average low of 14.6°C (58°F) (averages for 2015-2024).

What is the wettest month in Amsterdam?
October, with about 92 mm (3.6 in) of rain. The driest month is April.
