Practical weather notes for Bogotá

Bogotá's high altitude gives it a cool climate year-round despite its near-equatorial location, with daytime temperatures rarely swinging far in either direction. Afternoon rain and thunderstorms are common and can arrive quickly, even after a clear morning.

Seasons in Bogotá

There isn't a strong hot-and-cold seasonal cycle here; instead the year splits into wetter and drier stretches, with April–May and October–November usually the rainiest. Mornings tend to be the most reliably dry part of the day.

Best time to visit Bogotá

The warmest month in Bogotá is February, with an average high of 20.4°C (69°F). The coolest is July, at 18.4°C (65°F). The wettest month is March, with about 147 mm (5.8 in) of rain. Average highs stay below 25°C (77°F) all year.

Climate FAQ

What is the warmest month in Bogotá?
February, with an average high of 20.4°C (69°F) and an average low of 9.2°C (49°F) (averages for 2015-2024).

What is the wettest month in Bogotá?
March, with about 147 mm (5.8 in) of rain. The driest month is August.
