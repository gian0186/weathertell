Practical weather notes for Levi

Levi is the largest ski resort in Finland, about 140 kilometers north of the Arctic Circle. The climate is continental and cold, with dry snow and regular spells of -20°C (-4°F) or colder in winter. On the fell top the wind can make it feel colder than in the village, while on calm nights cold air can pool in the valley.

Seasons in Levi

The ski season usually runs from late October or November into May. From mid-December to the end of the month the sun does not rise above the horizon, and the days around it are mostly twilight. From March the days lengthen quickly and are often bright and sunny.

Best time to visit Levi

The warmest month in Levi is July, with an average high of 18.6°C (65°F). The coolest is January, at -8.2°C (17°F). The wettest month is July, with about 102 mm (4.0 in) of rain. Average highs stay below 25°C (77°F) all year. For sightseeing and walking, with highs between 18 and 27°C (64 to 81°F), July are the most comfortable.

Climate FAQ

What is the warmest month in Levi?
July, with an average high of 18.6°C (65°F) and an average low of 10.9°C (52°F) (averages for 2015-2024).

What is the wettest month in Levi?
July, with about 102 mm (4.0 in) of rain. The driest month is March.
