Practical weather notes for Munich

Munich sits close enough to the Alps that it experiences the Foehn effect: a warm, dry wind that descends the northern side of the mountains and can push temperatures up sharply and visibility to unusually clear levels within a matter of hours, even in winter. Locals often notice the mountains looking strikingly close on Foehn days.

Seasons in Munich

Winters are cold and can bring reliable snow, while summers are warm with a real risk of sudden, heavy thunderstorms in the afternoon, particularly in July and August. Spring can be notably changeable, sometimes swinging between warm sunshine and a late cold snap within the same week.

Best time to visit Munich

The warmest month in Munich is July, with an average high of 24.3°C (76°F). The coolest is January, at 4.1°C (39°F). The wettest month is May, with about 130 mm (5.1 in) of rain. Average highs stay below 25°C (77°F) all year. For sightseeing and walking, with highs between 18 and 27°C (64 to 81°F), June to September are the most comfortable.

Climate FAQ

What is the warmest month in Munich?
July, with an average high of 24.3°C (76°F) and an average low of 14.3°C (58°F) (averages for 2015-2024).

What is the wettest month in Munich?
May, with about 130 mm (5.1 in) of rain. The driest month is March.
