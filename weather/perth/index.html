Practical weather notes for Perth

Perth's isolated coastal position on the Indian Ocean gives it one of the sunniest climates of any major Australian city, with a reliable afternoon sea breeze, locally called the Fremantle Doctor, that takes the edge off summer heat. Inland suburbs tend to run noticeably hotter than those closer to the coast.

Seasons in Perth

Southern Hemisphere seasons apply: December to February is hot, dry summer, while June to August is the cooler, wetter winter, when most of the year's rain falls. Spring and autumn are generally mild, dry and pleasant for being outdoors.

Best time to visit Perth

The warmest month in Perth is February, with an average high of 30.3°C (87°F). The coolest is July, at 17.4°C (63°F). The wettest month is July, with about 117 mm (4.6 in) of rain. Highs of 25°C (77°F) or more are typical from November to March. For sightseeing and walking, with highs between 18 and 27°C (64 to 81°F), April to June and September to November are the most comfortable. The sea is warmest in February, at 24.3°C (76°F), and coolest in July, at 16.9°C (62°F). It is above 21°C (70°F), comfortable for swimming, from November to April.

Climate FAQ

What is the warmest month in Perth?
February, with an average high of 30.3°C (87°F) and an average low of 19.1°C (66°F) (averages for 2015-2024).

What is the wettest month in Perth?
July, with about 117 mm (4.6 in) of rain. The driest month is December.

When can you swim in the sea in Perth?
The sea is above 21°C (70°F) from November to April, and warmest in February at 24.3°C (76°F).
