Practical weather notes for New York

New York's position on the eastern seaboard means it can be affected by both cold continental air from the north and warm, humid air from the Atlantic, which is part of why the weather here can shift quickly. Summer humidity often makes the temperature feel several degrees higher than the number on the forecast.

Seasons in New York

Winters bring genuine cold and regular snow, while summers can turn hot and sticky for days at a time. Spring and autumn tend to be the most changeable seasons, worth checking hour-by-hour before heading out.

Frequently asked questions

How often is the forecast updated?
The live weather data refreshes several times a day, so you're always seeing the latest forecast for New York.

Is the 14-day forecast reliable?
The first few days are usually the most accurate. Further ahead, the forecast mainly shows a trend rather than the exact weather at a specific hour.

What should I check before heading outside?
Look at the combination of rain, wind and feels-like temperature. A short trip can handle a passing shower, but a longer walk or bike ride benefits from a more stable stretch of weather.

Best time to visit New York

The warmest month in New York is July, with an average high of 29.5°C (85°F). The coolest is January, at 4.4°C (40°F). The wettest month is July, with about 139 mm (5.5 in) of rain. Highs of 25°C (77°F) or more are typical from June to August. For sightseeing and walking, with highs between 18 and 27°C (64 to 81°F), May, June, September and October are the most comfortable. The sea is warmest in July, at 23.9°C (75°F), and coolest in February, at 4.3°C (40°F). It is above 21°C (70°F), comfortable for swimming, from July to September.

Climate FAQ

What is the warmest month in New York?
July, with an average high of 29.5°C (85°F) and an average low of 20.7°C (69°F) (averages for 2015-2024).

What is the wettest month in New York?
July, with about 139 mm (5.5 in) of rain. The driest month is November.

When can you swim in the sea in New York?
The sea is above 21°C (70°F) from July to September, and warmest in July at 23.9°C (75°F).
