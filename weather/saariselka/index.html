Practical weather notes for Saariselkä

Saariselkä lies in the north of Finnish Lapland, on the edge of Urho Kekkonen National Park and far from any big city, which makes it one of the darkest places to watch the northern lights. Winters are severely cold, often -20°C (-4°F) or lower, and the snow is usually dry and powdery.

Seasons in Saariselkä

Winter is long, with snow on the ground from about October into May. Around the winter solstice the sun stays below the horizon for about a month, although there are a few hours of twilight around midday. From March, sunny days make it ideal for cross-country skiing.

Best time to visit Saariselkä

The warmest month in Saariselkä is July, with an average high of 17.2°C (63°F). The coolest is January, at -8.6°C (17°F). The wettest month is August, with about 96 mm (3.8 in) of rain. Average highs stay below 25°C (77°F) all year.

Climate FAQ

What is the warmest month in Saariselkä?
July, with an average high of 17.2°C (63°F) and an average low of 10.2°C (50°F) (averages for 2015-2024).

What is the wettest month in Saariselkä?
