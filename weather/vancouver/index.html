Practical weather notes for Vancouver

Vancouver's mild, wet coastal climate is shaped by the Pacific Ocean and the mountains that surround the city, which trap rain-bearing clouds coming in from the sea. Persistent grey, drizzly stretches are more common than heavy downpours.

Seasons in Vancouver

Winters are mild for Canada, with rain rather than snow being the main feature at low elevation, while summers are dry, mild and considered some of the most pleasant weather in the country. Rain gear is more useful here than heavy winter clothing for most of the year.

Best time to visit Vancouver

The warmest month in Vancouver is August, with an average high of 22.5°C (73°F). The coolest is December, at 5.6°C (42°F). The wettest month is November, with about 315 mm (12.4 in) of rain. Average highs stay below 25°C (77°F) all year. For sightseeing and walking, with highs between 18 and 27°C (64 to 81°F), June to September are the most comfortable. The sea is warmest in August, at 19.2°C (67°F), and coolest in February, at 6.7°C (44°F). It doesn't reach 21°C (70°F) in any month.

Climate FAQ

What is the warmest month in Vancouver?
August, with an average high of 22.5°C (73°F) and an average low of 15.3°C (60°F) (averages for 2015-2024).

What is the wettest month in Vancouver?
November, with about 315 mm (12.4 in) of rain. The driest month is July.

When can you swim in the sea in Vancouver?
The sea stays below 21°C (70°F) on average; it is warmest in August, at 19.2°C (67°F).
