Practical weather notes for Philadelphia

Philadelphia sits in a transitional climate zone between the humid subtropical conditions further south and the colder continental climate further north, giving it four clearly distinct seasons. Summers are hot and humid, with the kind of sticky heat more associated with cities further south, while winters bring a mix of cold snaps, occasional snow and milder thaws.

Seasons in Philadelphia

Spring and autumn are generally mild and are considered the most comfortable seasons for being outdoors, though spring in particular can be changeable, swinging from warm sunshine to a late cold spell within the same week.

Best time to visit Philadelphia

The warmest month in Philadelphia is July, with an average high of 30.9°C (88°F). The coolest is January, at 5.4°C (42°F). The wettest month is July, with about 120 mm (4.7 in) of rain. Highs of 25°C (77°F) or more are typical from June to September. For sightseeing and walking, with highs between 18 and 27°C (64 to 81°F), May, September and October are the most comfortable.

Climate FAQ

What is the warmest month in Philadelphia?
July, with an average high of 30.9°C (88°F) and an average low of 21.1°C (70°F) (averages for 2015-2024).

What is the wettest month in Philadelphia?
July, with about 120 mm (4.7 in) of rain. The driest month is November.
