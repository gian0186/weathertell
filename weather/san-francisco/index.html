Practical weather notes for San Francisco

San Francisco has a famously localised climate, where fog rolling in off the cold Pacific can keep one neighbourhood cool and grey while another just a few kilometres inland stays warm and sunny on the same afternoon. Summer is often cooler and foggier than spring or autumn, which can surprise first-time visitors.

Seasons in San Francisco

Rain is concentrated in the winter months, while summer stays dry but can still feel chilly near the coast because of the fog. September and October are usually the warmest, sunniest stretch of the year.

Best time to visit San Francisco

The warmest month in San Francisco is September, with an average high of 21.2°C (70°F). The coolest is December, at 13.5°C (56°F). The wettest month is December, with about 129 mm (5.1 in) of rain. Average highs stay below 25°C (77°F) all year. For sightseeing and walking, with highs between 18 and 27°C (64 to 81°F), June to October are the most comfortable. The sea is warmest in September, at 16.6°C (62°F), and coolest in January, at 12.2°C (54°F). It doesn't reach 21°C (70°F) in any month.

Climate FAQ

What is the warmest month in San Francisco?
September, with an average high of 21.2°C (70°F) and an average low of 14.3°C (58°F) (averages for 2015-2024).

What is the wettest month in San Francisco?
December, with about 129 mm (5.1 in) of rain. The driest month is June.

When can you swim in the sea in San Francisco?
The sea stays below 21°C (70°F) on average; it is warmest in September, at 16.6°C (62°F).
