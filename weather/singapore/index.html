Practical weather notes for Singapore

Singapore's position near the equator means temperature stays warm and humid all year, with very little seasonal variation. Short, heavy downpours can arrive suddenly even on an otherwise sunny day, so it's worth checking the hour-by-hour forecast more than the daily one.

Seasons in Singapore

There is no traditional summer or winter here — instead the year is shaped by two monsoon seasons, roughly November to March and June to September, which bring somewhat more frequent rain than the drier months between them.

Frequently asked questions

How often is the forecast updated?
The live weather data refreshes several times a day, so you're always seeing the latest forecast for Singapore.

Is the 14-day forecast reliable?
The first few days are usually the most accurate. Further ahead, the forecast mainly shows a trend rather than the exact weather at a specific hour.

What should I check before heading outside?
Look at the combination of rain, wind and feels-like temperature. A short trip can handle a passing shower, but a longer walk or bike ride benefits from a more stable stretch of weather.

Best time to visit Singapore

The warmest month in Singapore is March, with an average high of 30.4°C (87°F). The coolest is January, at 28.9°C (84°F). The wettest month is November, with about 382 mm (15.0 in) of rain. Highs of 25°C (77°F) or more are typical all year round. The sea is warmest in May, at 30.9°C (88°F), and coolest in January, at 28.2°C (83°F). It is above 21°C (70°F), comfortable for swimming, all year round.

Climate FAQ

What is the warmest month in Singapore?
March, with an average high of 30.4°C (87°F) and an average low of 24.2°C (76°F) (averages for 2015-2024).

What is the wettest month in Singapore?
November, with about 382 mm (15.0 in) of rain. The driest month is February.

When can you swim in the sea in Singapore?
The sea is above 21°C (70°F) all year round, and warmest in May at 30.9°C (88°F).
