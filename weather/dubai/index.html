Practical weather notes for Dubai

Dubai's desert climate means summers are extremely hot, often too hot for comfortable outdoor activity in the middle of the day, while winters are warm and pleasant by most standards. Humidity near the coast can make summer heat feel even more intense than the temperature alone suggests.

Seasons in Dubai

Rain is rare in any season and, when it happens, is usually confined to a few days in winter. Late autumn through early spring is generally considered the most comfortable stretch for being outdoors.

What should I check before heading outside?
Look at the combination of rain, wind and feels-like temperature. A short trip can handle a passing shower, but a longer walk or bike ride benefits from a more stable stretch of weather.

Best time to visit Dubai

The warmest month in Dubai is July, with an average high of 41.1°C (106°F). The coolest is January, at 24.1°C (75°F). The wettest month is January, with about 23 mm (0.9 in) of rain. Highs of 25°C (77°F) or more are typical from February to December. For sightseeing and walking, with highs between 18 and 27°C (64 to 81°F), December to February are the most comfortable. The sea is warmest in July, at 34.5°C (94°F), and coolest in February, at 21.8°C (71°F). It is above 21°C (70°F), comfortable for swimming, all year round.

Climate FAQ

What is the warmest month in Dubai?
July, with an average high of 41.1°C (106°F) and an average low of 30.6°C (87°F) (averages for 2015-2024).

What is the wettest month in Dubai?
January, with about 23 mm (0.9 in) of rain. The driest month is June.

When can you swim in the sea in Dubai?
The sea is above 21°C (70°F) all year round, and warmest in July at 34.5°C (94°F).
