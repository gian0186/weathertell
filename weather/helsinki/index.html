Practical weather notes for Helsinki

Helsinki's northern position means daylight swings dramatically between seasons, shaping the weather as much as temperature does. The nearby Baltic Sea moderates temperatures somewhat compared with inland Finland, but wind off the water is a near-constant factor.

Seasons in Helsinki

Winters are cold with reliable snow and long dark stretches, while summers are mild rather than hot, with very long daylight hours that make even average temperatures feel pleasant outdoors.

Frequently asked questions

How often is the forecast updated?
The live weather data refreshes several times a day, so you're always seeing the latest forecast for Helsinki.

Is the 14-day forecast reliable?
The first few days are usually the most accurate. Further ahead, the forecast mainly shows a trend rather than the exact weather at a specific hour.

What should I check before heading outside?
Look at the combination of rain, wind and feels-like temperature. A short trip can handle a passing shower, but a longer walk or bike ride benefits from a more stable stretch of weather.
