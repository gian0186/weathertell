Practical weather notes for Barcelona

Barcelona's coastal position keeps summer heat gentler than inland Spanish cities, with a sea breeze that regularly cools the afternoon near the beach. Humidity can still make hot days feel heavier than the temperature alone suggests, especially in the narrow streets of the old town.

Seasons in Barcelona

Summers are long, warm and mostly dry, while winters are mild with rain being the main thing to plan for rather than cold. Spring and autumn are generally the most comfortable seasons for walking the city.

What should I check before heading outside?
Look at the combination of rain, wind and feels-like temperature. A short trip can handle a passing shower, but a longer walk or bike ride benefits from a more stable stretch of weather.

Best time to visit Barcelona

The warmest month in Barcelona is July, with an average high of 28.7°C (84°F). The coolest is January, at 13.5°C (56°F). The wettest month is October, with about 105 mm (4.1 in) of rain. Highs of 25°C (77°F) or more are typical from June to September. For sightseeing and walking, with highs between 18 and 27°C (64 to 81°F), May, June, September and October are the most comfortable. The sea is warmest in August, at 27.4°C (81°F), and coolest in February, at 13.6°C (56°F). It is above 21°C (70°F), comfortable for swimming, from June to October.

Climate FAQ

What is the warmest month in Barcelona?
July, with an average high of 28.7°C (84°F) and an average low of 21.5°C (71°F) (averages for 2015-2024).

What is the wettest month in Barcelona?
October, with about 105 mm (4.1 in) of rain. The driest month is December.

When can you swim in the sea in Barcelona?
The sea is above 21°C (70°F) from June to October, and warmest in August at 27.4°C (81°F).
