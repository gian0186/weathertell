Practical weather notes for Jakarta

Jakarta's equatorial position means warm, humid weather all year with little seasonal temperature variation. Afternoon thunderstorms are common and can bring sudden, heavy rainfall that floods low-lying streets quickly, so it's worth checking the hour-by-hour forecast before heading out.

Seasons in Jakarta

The wet season runs roughly from November to March, bringing the heaviest and most frequent rain, while June to September is comparatively drier, though rarely fully dry. Temperature stays warm in every month.

What should I check before heading outside?
Look at the combination of rain, wind and feels-like temperature. A short trip can handle a passing shower, but a longer walk or bike ride benefits from a more stable stretch of weather.

Best time to visit Jakarta

The warmest month in Jakarta is September, with an average high of 32.6°C (91°F). The coolest is February, at 29.5°C (85°F). The wettest month is February, with about 321 mm (12.6 in) of rain. Highs of 25°C (77°F) or more are typical all year round. The sea is warmest in May, at 31.1°C (88°F), and coolest in January, at 29.6°C (85°F). It is above 21°C (70°F), comfortable for swimming, all year round.

Climate FAQ

What is the warmest month in Jakarta?
September, with an average high of 32.6°C (91°F) and an average low of 23.6°C (74°F) (averages for 2015-2024).

What is the wettest month in Jakarta?
February, with about 321 mm (12.6 in) of rain. The driest month is August.

When can you swim in the sea in Jakarta?
The sea is above 21°C (70°F) all year round, and warmest in May at 31.1°C (88°F).
