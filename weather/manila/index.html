Practical weather notes for Manila

Manila's tropical climate means heat and humidity are a near-constant backdrop, with the feels-like temperature often well above the actual reading. Afternoon thunderstorms are common in the wet season and can flood streets quickly.

Seasons in Manila

The wet season runs roughly from June to November, including typhoon season, while the dry season from December to May is hotter and sunnier. Even the coolest months stay warm by most standards.

Frequently asked questions

Is the 14-day forecast reliable?
The first few days are usually the most accurate. Further ahead, the forecast mainly shows a trend rather than the exact weather at a specific hour.

What should I check before heading outside?
Look at the combination of rain, wind and feels-like temperature. A short trip can handle a passing shower, but a longer walk or bike ride benefits from a more stable stretch of weather.

Best time to visit Manila

The warmest month in Manila is April, with an average high of 33.4°C (92°F). The coolest is January, at 29.0°C (84°F). The wettest month is July, with about 403 mm (15.9 in) of rain. Highs of 25°C (77°F) or more are typical all year round. The sea is warmest in May, at 31.9°C (89°F), and coolest in February, at 27.8°C (82°F). It is above 21°C (70°F), comfortable for swimming, all year round.

Climate FAQ

What is the warmest month in Manila?
April, with an average high of 33.4°C (92°F) and an average low of 25.4°C (78°F) (averages for 2015-2024).

What is the wettest month in Manila?
July, with about 403 mm (15.9 in) of rain. The driest month is March.

When can you swim in the sea in Manila?
The sea is above 21°C (70°F) all year round, and warmest in May at 31.9°C (89°F).
