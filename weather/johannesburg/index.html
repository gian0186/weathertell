Practical weather notes for Johannesburg

Johannesburg's high altitude keeps temperatures milder than its latitude might suggest, with warm days and noticeably cool nights, even in summer. Afternoon thunderstorms are a regular summer feature and can build quickly, often clearing within an hour or two.

Seasons in Johannesburg

Southern Hemisphere seasons apply: November to March is the warm, wet summer with frequent afternoon storms, while June to August is the dry, sunny winter with cold nights and occasional frost. Spring and autumn are generally mild, dry and comfortable for being outdoors.

Best time to visit Johannesburg

The warmest month in Johannesburg is October, with an average high of 26.1°C (79°F). The coolest is June, at 17.1°C (63°F). The wettest month is January, with about 117 mm (4.6 in) of rain. Highs of 25°C (77°F) or more are typical from October to February. For sightseeing and walking, with highs between 18 and 27°C (64 to 81°F), August to May are the most comfortable.

Climate FAQ

What is the warmest month in Johannesburg?
October, with an average high of 26.1°C (79°F) and an average low of 11.7°C (53°F) (averages for 2015-2024).

What is the wettest month in Johannesburg?
January, with about 117 mm (4.6 in) of rain. The driest month is August.
