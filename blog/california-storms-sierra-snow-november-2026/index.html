California Storms: Rain, Sierra Snow and a Wet Week Ahead

By Emma Clarke · November 15, 2026

The storm season has started on the West Coast. A series of Pacific storms is bringing rain to Northern California, heavy snow to the Sierra Nevada and dangerous surf to the coast, and the wet pattern looks set to last into Thanksgiving week.

Photo: rain in San Francisco, Sergiy Galyonkin, Wikimedia Commons (CC BY-SA 2.0)

Key facts
  • Now: the first big storm of the season brought rain to the Bay Area and the first accumulating snow to the Sierra on Friday and Saturday; a second round arrives Sunday into Monday.
  • Snow: a Winter Storm Warning is in effect for parts of the Sierra Nevada, with up to 20 inches of additional snow possible before Tuesday morning, according to the National Weather Service.
  • Rain: San Francisco gets about 1.8 inches from Sunday to Saturday in the European model, close to the 2.3 inches that normally falls in all of November; Wednesday is the wettest day.
  • Next: after a break on Tuesday, a stronger storm brings rain and wind on Wednesday and Thursday.
  • Thanksgiving: another wet spell is likely around November 25 to 27, with rain in most model runs for the Bay Area.

What is happening now?

The first big storm of the season reached Northern California on Friday, November 13, bringing rain and high surf to the Bay Area and the first accumulating snow to the Sierra. Forecasters expected 6 to 12 inches of snow at lake level around Lake Tahoe and more than 18 inches on the highest peaks, with 1 to 2 feet above 7,000 feet in the Eastern Sierra, according to the San Francisco Chronicle.

A second round arrives on Sunday, November 15, with rain in the valleys and foothills in the afternoon and snow in the Sierra overnight. The snow can be heavy at times into Monday morning. The National Weather Service has a Winter Storm Warning in effect for parts of the Sierra Nevada, with up to 20 inches of additional snow possible before it expires on Tuesday morning. Travel over the mountain passes, including Interstate 80 over Donner Pass, can be difficult or impossible during the heaviest snow; check chain controls before you go.

How much rain will fall this week?

After a short break on Tuesday, a stronger storm brings more rain and stronger winds on Wednesday and Thursday, November 18 and 19. In the European ECMWF model, Wednesday is the wettest day of the week in the Bay Area, with close to an inch of rain in San Francisco. Southern California gets much less: most runs bring only light rain to Los Angeles and San Diego, but a few bring a soaking.

For San Francisco, 1.8 inches in one week is close to the 2.3 inches that normally falls in all of November. Over the two weeks to November 28, the expected 3.7 inches is well above a normal November. In Los Angeles, where November normally brings about 0.8 inches, the outcome depends on how far south the storms reach. Seattle and Portland get a wet but fairly ordinary week for the time of year; Seattle normally sees almost 7 inches in November. Many of these storms are fed by long bands of moisture from the Pacific; how those work is explained in what is an atmospheric river.

Will it be wet for Thanksgiving?

Probably, at least in Northern California. After a drier spell around November 21 to 24, the European model brings another round of rain around November 25 to 27. In our Thanksgiving 2026 forecast, about 7 in 10 of the 82 runs of the European and American models bring rain to San Francisco on Thanksgiving Day, against a normal of about 1 in 4. If you are driving over the Sierra for the holiday, keep an eye on the forecast: new snow on the passes is possible. Follow your own city in the 14-day forecast.

Staying safe in the storms

Dangerous surf is the biggest risk on the coast: large waves can sweep people off rocks and jetties, so stay well back from the water. Heavy rain on dry ground can cause flooding on roads and in low-lying areas, and burn scars from past wildfires are especially prone to mud and debris flows. In the mountains, carry chains, extra water and warm clothes, and avoid travel during the heaviest snow. Gusty winds can also bring down trees and power lines.

Frequently asked questions

How much snow will the Sierra get?
The first storm brought 6 to 12 inches at lake level around Lake Tahoe and 1 to 2 feet above 7,000 feet in the Eastern Sierra. The National Weather Service expects up to 20 inches of additional snow in parts of the Sierra Nevada before Tuesday morning.

How much rain will San Francisco get this week?
About 1.8 inches from Sunday to Saturday in the European model, with a likely range of 1.1 to 3.2 inches. That is close to the 2.3 inches that normally falls in all of November. Wednesday is the wettest day.

Will it rain in Los Angeles?
Some rain is possible on Wednesday and Thursday, but most model runs bring only light amounts, about 0.4 inches for the week. A few runs bring a soaking, so check the forecast closer to the day.

Will it rain on Thanksgiving in California?
In Northern California it looks likely. About 7 in 10 runs of the European and American models bring rain to San Francisco on Thanksgiving Day, November 26.
