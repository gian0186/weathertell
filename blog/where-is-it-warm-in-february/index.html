Where Is It Warm in February? 26 Winter Sun Destinations Compared

By Emma Clarke · November 14, 2026

February is the last full winter month in the northern hemisphere, with average highs of 6°C (43°F) in New York and 9°C (48°F) in London. For many destinations in the tropics it is one of the best months of the year: dry, sunny and hot. We compared 26 winter sun destinations on their average February temperatures and sea temperatures.

Photo: Ipanema and Dois Irmãos, Rio de Janeiro, by Mteixeira62, Wikimedia Commons (CC BY-SA 3.0)

Key facts
  • Hottest: Costa Rica's Guanacaste coast (33°C, 91°F), Phuket (31°C, 88°F) and Rio de Janeiro (30°C, 86°F).
  • Driest: February is one of the driest months in Guanacaste, Phuket and on the Caribbean islands.
  • US: Miami warms up again to 25°C (77°F), Phoenix to 21°C (70°F).
  • From the UK: Dubai (25°C, 77°F) and Cape Verde (24°C, 75°F); the Canary Islands and Hurghada reach 21 to 22°C (70 to 72°F).
  • Carnival: Mardi Gras falls on February 9, 2027; Rio's carnival parades run from February 5.

Hot and dry: Costa Rica and Thailand

The Guanacaste coast of Costa Rica is at its hottest and driest in February, with an average high of 33°C (91°F) and a sea of 29°C (84°F). Phuket averages 31°C (88°F), and February is one of the driest and sunniest months on Thailand's Andaman coast, with a calm sea of 29°C (84°F). Bali is just as warm, at 29°C (84°F), but still in the middle of its wet season.

Carnival in Rio and Mardi Gras

In 2027 Carnival falls early: Mardi Gras, or Shrove Tuesday, is on February 9, the day before Ash Wednesday. In Rio de Janeiro, the parades in the Sambadrome start on Friday, February 5. It is midsummer there, with an average high of 30°C (86°F), nights of 24°C (75°F) and a sea of about 28°C (82°F). It is hot and humid, with a good chance of a short, heavy afternoon thunderstorm, so plan the beach for the morning. In New Orleans, Mardi Gras weather is much less predictable: anything from spring warmth to a cold, rainy day is possible in February.

The Caribbean and Mexico in February

The Caribbean is in its driest period in February. Aruba averages 28°C (82°F), Barbados 28°C (82°F) and Montego Bay 28°C (82°F), with a sea of about 26 to 28°C (79 to 82°F). Punta Cana reaches 27°C (81°F) and Nassau 25°C (77°F).

In Mexico, Cancún averages 27°C (81°F) and gets noticeably drier than in December. Puerto Vallarta (26°C, 79°F) and Cabo San Lucas (26°C, 79°F) are dry and sunny, and February is one of the best months for whale watching off Baja California. The sea at Cabo is at its coolest now, about 21°C (70°F).

Warm weather in the US in February

Miami warms up again after January, to an average of 25°C (77°F), with a sea of about 24°C (75°F). February is still part of the dry season in South Florida. Key West averages 23°C (73°F) and Orlando 24°C (75°F). Honolulu stays warm at 25°C (77°F). In Phoenix the warmth returns, with an average high of 21°C (70°F), while San Diego stays around 18°C (64°F).

Winter sun from the UK in February half-term

For a February half-term break from the UK, Dubai is again the warmest of the classic options, with an average high of 25°C (77°F), little rain and a sea of about 22°C (72°F). Cape Verde (Sal) reaches 24°C (75°F). The south of Tenerife (21°C, 70°F) and Hurghada (22°C, 72°F) are closer, but February is one of the coolest months there, with a sea of about 21°C (70°F). Marrakech warms up to 21°C (70°F) during the day.

Late summer in Sydney and Cape Town

Sydney is still in summer, with an average high of 26°C (79°F) and the warmest sea of the year at about 24°C (75°F), but February is also one of its wetter months. Cape Town is warm and dry, with highs of 26°C (79°F), the wine harvest under way and an Atlantic that stays cold at about 15°C (59°F). Planning an earlier trip? Frequently asked questions

Where is it warm in February in the US?
In Hawaii and South Florida. Honolulu averages 25°C (77°F) and Miami 25°C (77°F) in February. Phoenix warms up to 21°C (70°F), sunny but with cool nights.

Where is it hottest in February?
On Costa Rica's Pacific coast (33°C, 91°F), in Phuket (31°C, 88°F) and in Rio de Janeiro (30°C, 86°F). The Caribbean islands average about 28°C (82°F).

When is Mardi Gras 2027?
Mardi Gras, or Shrove Tuesday, falls on February 9, 2027, the day before Ash Wednesday. In Rio de Janeiro the Sambadrome parades start on Friday, February 5.

Where is it warm in February for a half-term break from the UK?
Dubai (25°C, 77°F) and Cape Verde (24°C, 75°F) are the warmest options within about seven hours. The Canary Islands and Egypt's Red Sea coast reach 21 to 22°C (70 to 72°F), pleasant but not hot.
