How Cold Will It Be on Halloween 2026?

By Emma Clarke · October 1, 2026

Halloween falls on a Saturday in 2026, which usually means more trick-or-treaters out for longer. We looked at 36 years of weather data for New York (1990-2025) to see what October 31 typically brings, and how dark it already is by the time the first doorbells start ringing.

Key facts
  • Typical weather: Based on 36 years of New York data, October 31 averages a high around 15°C (59°F) and a low near 7°C (44°F).
  • Rain chance: Measurable rain has fallen on Halloween in New York in about 1 year in 5 over the last 36 years.
  • Darkness: In New York the sun sets around 5:53pm on October 31, so most trick-or-treating happens in fading or full darkness.
  • Extremes: The warmest Halloween was 27.6°C (82°F) in 2024; the coldest, in 2011, had a high of just 6.7°C (44°F).
  • Tip: Layer warm clothing such as thermals, leggings or a hoodie under the costume rather than a bulky coat over it.

How cold does it usually get on Halloween?

Based on 36 years of data for New York (1990-2025), the average high on October 31 is around 15°C (59°F), with the average low closer to 7°C (44°F). That is cool enough that a costume alone rarely cuts it once the sun goes down, and most years call for a jacket layer underneath. Measurable rain has fallen on this date in about 1 year in 5 over that period, so it is dry far more often than not, but worth a glance at the forecast before heading out.

How dark is it by trick-or-treat time?

In New York, the sun sets around 5:53pm on October 31, with dusk settling in over the following half hour. Since Halloween 2026 falls before clocks change for daylight saving time, trick-or-treaters heading out in the early evening will already be doing most of their rounds in the dark. A flashlight or light-up accessory is worth packing regardless of the forecast, since visibility matters as much as the weather once the sun is down.

Dressing for both the costume and the cold

The classic trick-or-treat problem is that a costume and a warm coat rarely work well together. Layering underneath, rather than over, keeps the costume visible while still adding warmth: thermal layers, leggings or an extra hoodie worn under a costume tend to work better than a bulky coat on top of it. Checking the evening temperature specifically, not just the daytime high, is the more useful number here, since trick-or-treating typically happens well after the warmest part of the day has passed.

Checking closer to the date

A 36-year average is useful for knowing roughly what to pack, but it is not a forecast for this specific Halloween. What time does it get dark on Halloween?
