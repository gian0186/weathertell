Where Is It Warm in December? 26 Winter Sun Destinations Compared

By Emma Clarke · October 17, 2026

In December the average high in New York drops to about 7°C (45°F) and in London to 9°C (48°F), and the days are at their shortest. If you would rather spend the holidays in the sun, there is plenty of choice, but the differences between destinations are bigger than many people expect. We compared 26 winter sun destinations on their average December temperatures and sea temperatures.

Photo: Lanikai Beach, Oahu, by claralieu, Wikimedia Commons (CC BY 2.0)

Key facts
  • Hottest: Costa Rica's Guanacaste coast (31°C, 88°F), Phuket, Bali and Rio de Janeiro reach about 29°C (84°F) or more, but Bali and Rio are in their rainy season.
  • Caribbean: Aruba, Barbados, Jamaica and Puerto Rico average about 28°C (82°F), with sea temperatures of 28°C (82°F) or more.
  • Closest from the US: Miami (25°C, 77°F), Key West and Honolulu stay in the mid to upper 70s °F; Orlando and Phoenix are milder, around 19 to 23°C (66 to 73°F).
  • From the UK: Dubai (26°C, 79°F) and Cape Verde (26°C, 79°F) are summer-warm; the Canary Islands and Egypt's Red Sea coast reach about 22°C (72°F).
  • Watch out: Cape Town is in summer, but the Atlantic there is only about 15°C (59°F).

Tropical heat: Costa Rica, Thailand and the southern Caribbean

The warmest place in our list is the Guanacaste coast of Costa Rica, with an average high of 31°C (88°F). December is the start of the dry season there, and the Pacific is around 29°C (84°F). Phuket comes next at 29°C (84°F), with a sea just as warm; December is the start of the dry season on Thailand's Andaman coast, although a tropical shower is still possible early in the month.

In the Caribbean, Aruba (29°C, 84°F), Barbados, Montego Bay in Jamaica and San Juan in Puerto Rico all average around 28°C (82°F), with nights that rarely drop below 22°C (72°F). The Atlantic hurricane season officially ends on November 30, and December is the start of the drier, breezier winter season, although short showers remain common. Aruba sits outside the usual hurricane belt and is one of the driest islands in the region.

Hot but wet: Bali and Rio de Janeiro

Two destinations look great on temperature alone but come with a catch. Bali averages 29°C (84°F), but December falls in its wet season, which runs from about November to March: expect heavy afternoon downpours on many days. Rio de Janeiro is in early summer, with highs of 29°C (84°F) and a sea of about 25°C (77°F), but summer is also its rainiest season, with frequent thunderstorms. Both are still popular, as the showers often pass quickly, but they are not the place for guaranteed sunshine.

Mexico and the Bahamas: warm and a short flight away

For travelers from the US and Canada, Mexico is one of the easiest ways to find warmth. Puerto Vallarta (27°C, 81°F) and Cabo San Lucas (27°C, 81°F) on the Pacific side are in their dry season, with almost no rain. Cancún on the Caribbean coast averages 27°C (81°F) with a sea of 28°C (82°F), warm enough to swim. In winter it is occasionally hit by a norte, a cold front from the north that brings a few days of wind, clouds and cooler air. Punta Cana in the Dominican Republic averages 27°C (81°F), and Nassau in the Bahamas 26°C (79°F), a little cooler than the islands further south.

Warm weather in the US: Florida, Hawaii and the Southwest

Within the US, Miami is the classic choice, with an average December high of 25°C (77°F) and a sea of about 26°C (79°F). December is part of its dry season, although a passing cold front can bring a few cooler days. Key West is similar, with the mildest nights in the continental US at about 22°C (72°F). Honolulu averages 26°C (79°F) with a sea of 26°C (79°F); winter is the wetter season in Hawaii and the season of big waves on the north shores.

Further inland it gets cooler. Orlando averages 23°C (73°F) during the day, but nights drop to 14°C (57°F). Phoenix is sunny and dry at 19°C (66°F), but with cold nights around 6°C (43°F). San Diego reaches about 18°C (64°F), pleasant for sightseeing, but with a Pacific of only 16°C (61°F) it is not beach weather.

Winter sun from the UK and Europe

From the UK, Dubai is the most reliable choice in December, with an average high of 26°C (79°F), hardly any rain and a sea of about 25°C (77°F). It is peak season there. Cape Verde (Sal) comes close at 26°C (79°F), with mild nights of 22°C (72°F) and a steady trade wind that makes it popular with kitesurfers.

For a shorter flight, the south of Tenerife averages 22°C (72°F) and Hurghada on Egypt's Red Sea coast 23°C (73°F), both with very little rain and a sea of about 22 to 24°C (72 to 75°F). Marrakech reaches 21°C (70°F) on sunny days, but nights can drop to 7°C (45°F).

Summer in the southern hemisphere: Sydney and Cape Town

In Sydney and Cape Town, December is the start of summer. Sydney averages 26°C (79°F) with a sea of 21°C (70°F), and its New Year's Eve fireworks over the harbor are a big draw. Cape Town is warm and very sunny at 24°C (75°F), but the cold Benguela current keeps the Atlantic at about 15°C (59°F) on the city's west-facing beaches. It can also be very windy: the strong southeasterly wind is so common in summer that locals call it the Cape Doctor.

Christmas and New Year in the sun

Christmas and New Year are the busiest weeks of the winter for sun destinations, so book early. Frequently asked questions

Where is it warm in December in the US?
In South Florida and Hawaii. Miami averages 25°C (77°F) in December and Honolulu 26°C (79°F), both with a sea of around 26°C (79°F). Phoenix and Orlando are milder, around 19 to 23°C (66 to 73°F) during the day, with cool nights.

Where is it warm for Christmas?
In the southern Caribbean (Aruba, Barbados, Jamaica, about 28°C or 83°F), on Costa Rica's Pacific coast (31°C, 88°F), in Thailand (29°C, 84°F) and in Dubai (26°C, 79°F). Closer to Europe, the Canary Islands reach about 22°C (72°F).

Can you swim in the sea in December?
Yes, in the tropics. The sea is about 28°C (82°F) around Aruba, Barbados, Jamaica, Puerto Rico and Costa Rica, 29°C (84°F) at Phuket and about 26°C (79°F) off Miami. Around the Canary Islands it is about 22°C (72°F), cool but fine for a swim. In San Diego and Cape Town the sea is too cold for most people.

Is December a good time to visit the Caribbean?
Yes. The hurricane season ends on November 30, and December is the start of the drier, breezier winter season. Short tropical showers are still common, but they usually pass quickly.
