Thanksgiving Storm 2026: Rain and Wind on the East Coast

By Emma Clarke · November 24, 2026

Thanksgiving week brings the busiest travel days of the year, and the weather is not cooperating on the East Coast. A strengthening coastal storm moves up the Eastern Seaboard just as the holiday rush peaks, with soaking rain, strong winds and coastal flooding from the Carolinas to New England.

Photo: rain in New York City, Christopher Michel, Wikimedia Commons (CC BY 2.0)

Key facts
  • When: rain reaches the Southeast on Tuesday, the Mid-Atlantic and New York on Thanksgiving Day, and New England on Thanksgiving night and Friday.
  • Rain: about 1.2 inches in Washington, D.C. and 0.6 to 0.8 inches in Philadelphia and New York on Thursday.
  • Wind: gusts of 45 to 50 mph from Washington to New York; the National Weather Service in Boston expects coastal gusts above 50 mph.
  • Travel: flight delays are most likely in Atlanta on Wednesday and along the I-95 corridor on Thursday; return trips on Friday may be slowed in the Northeast.
  • West: California is dry on Thanksgiving Day, but storms return on the weekend, with delays possible in California on Sunday.

Day by day: when the storm hits

The storm develops over the Southeast early in the week. On Tuesday, rain and gusty winds begin in Florida and the Southeast, and The Weather Channel expects weather-related flight delays in places such as Atlanta on Wednesday. On Thanksgiving Day the storm strengthens as it moves up the coast, bringing rain and strong winds to the Mid-Atlantic and New York. On Thanksgiving night and Friday it reaches New England, where the National Weather Service in Boston warns of a "potentially significant coastal storm" with coastal gusts possibly topping 50 mph.

How much rain and wind, city by city

The European and American weather models agree that the I-95 corridor gets a wet and windy Thanksgiving. Almost all of their 82 runs bring rain to Washington, Philadelphia and New York on Thursday.

Coastal flooding is possible at high tide along the Mid-Atlantic and New England coast, where onshore winds push water into bays and low-lying streets. Inland, the storm is mostly rain: snow is not expected in the big East Coast cities. For highs and rain chances in more cities, see our Thanksgiving 2026 forecast.

What it means for travel

Tuesday and especially Wednesday are among the busiest travel days of the year, and that rush overlaps with the storm moving up the coast. Expect delays at airports in the Southeast on Wednesday and along the I-95 corridor, from Washington to Boston, on Thursday. Strong crosswinds can slow down runways in New York and Boston even where rain is light. On the roads, heavy rain and wind make driving harder on I-95, and on Friday, return trips in the Northeast and the Ohio Valley may be slowed by leftover rain and, in places, a wintry mix.

If you can, travel early on Tuesday or wait until Saturday. Check your airline before you go, and follow the hourly forecast for your city in the 14-day forecast.

Will the Macy's parade go ahead?

The parade in New York goes ahead in almost any weather, but the giant balloons are flown lower or grounded when sustained winds top 23 mph or gusts top 34 mph. This year it depends on the timing of the storm. The European model keeps Thursday morning dry in Manhattan, with gusts below 30 mph; the American GFS model brings the rain and gusts above 45 mph in by late morning. The final decision on the balloons is made on the morning itself.

Meanwhile in the West

The West gets a break on the holiday itself. California is dry and mild on Thanksgiving Day, with sunshine in San Francisco and close to 80°F in Los Angeles. The next atmospheric river storms reach California and the Sierra Nevada over the weekend, with fresh mountain snow and "difficult travel" over the passes, according to the National Weather Service in Reno; The Weather Channel sees the best chance of flight delays in California on Sunday. Seattle is wet on Thursday. Read how these storms work in what is an atmospheric river.

Frequently asked questions

Will it rain on Thanksgiving in New York?
Very likely. Almost all runs of the European and American models bring rain to New York on Thanksgiving Day, about 0.6 inches, with gusts around 45 mph.

Will the Thanksgiving storm cause flight delays?
Delays are most likely in the Southeast, including Atlanta, on Wednesday and along the I-95 corridor from Washington to Boston on Thursday. Friday return trips in the Northeast may also be slowed.

Will the Macy's parade balloons fly?
It depends on the timing of the storm. Balloons are grounded or flown lower when sustained winds top 23 mph or gusts top 34 mph. The European model keeps the morning calmer, the American model brings stronger winds by late morning.

Will it snow on Thanksgiving?
Not in the big East Coast cities; the storm brings rain. A wintry mix is possible in parts of the Ohio Valley on Friday, and fresh snow falls in the Sierra Nevada over the weekend.
