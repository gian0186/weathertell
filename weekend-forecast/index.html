How the weekend forecast works

This page picks out Saturday and Sunday from the forecast for your city and compares them: the high and low, the chance of rain, the expected amount of rain, wind and hours of sunshine. On a Sunday it shows today and the following weekend. The comparison of the two days is a simple guide, based on the chance of rain and the hours of sunshine.

How far ahead is the weekend forecast reliable?

From Wednesday or Thursday the forecast for the weekend is usually quite reliable. Earlier in the week it mostly shows a trend: whether the weekend is likely to be warm or cool, wet or dry. Check again on Friday for the details, and look at the hourly forecast on the day itself to plan around a passing shower.
