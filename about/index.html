About WeatherTell

Many weather websites are crowded with ads and pop-ups, while most visitors want a simple answer: will it stay dry tomorrow, and what can I expect over the next two weeks? WeatherTell.com is built to answer that question as quickly as possible, for any place in the world, with a clear 14-day forecast, hourly details and monthly climate tables for many cities.

Our data

Our forecasts come from Open-Meteo, which combines the output of established weather models. Read how our forecast works on the 14-day forecast page. An overview of all our sources and licenses, from climate data to photos, is on the sources and licenses page.

The team

The articles on this site are written by Emma Clarke, who explains not just what the weather will do, but why. Figures in our articles come from named sources, which we credit in the text or under each table.

WeatherTell.com is a project of Gian Boender, who also runs the Dutch sister site Weersvooruitzicht.nl and builds Bugalou, customer service software that lets online stores answer WhatsApp, email and live chat from one inbox.
